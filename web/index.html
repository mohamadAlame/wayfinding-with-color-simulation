Before we start

  • What this is: a student project for a Human-Centered Design course at AUB, about how people find their way.
  • What you do: a short practice, then you listen to or read directions and find a building in Google Street View. About 8 minutes.
  • What is recorded: your answers, the route you take in Street View, how long you take, and whether you find the building. No name, audio or video. Street View is loaded from Google, which receives its usual technical data.
  • Voluntary: you can stop at any time without giving a reason.
  • Later: your data is stored under the code . Give us the code and we delete it.
  • We are testing the directions, not you.
