Find a building

You'll walk through a small 3D street and find a building. It takes about 5 minutes, on a phone or a computer.

  1. A short practice, to learn how to move.
  2. Directions, shown once.
  3. You walk and find the building.
  4. Three quick questions.

Before you start

  • This is a student project for a Human-Centered Design course at AUB, about how people find their way. We are testing the directions, not you.
  • We record your answers, the route you walk, how long it takes, which building you choose, and whether you use a phone or a computer. No name, email, camera or microphone.
  • It's voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page; what was recorded until then is kept unless you ask us to delete it.
  • You get a code, . With it, we can delete your data later. At the end we explain exactly what we were looking at, and you can delete it yourself.

What we were looking at

The buildings had large banners. For half of the people all the banners look the same, as they do today. For the other half each building's banner has its own colour, and the directions mention it. We want to know whether that makes a building easier to find.
