Find a building

Imagine you arrive at a hospital and ask the guard how to get to a building. Here you walk there in a 3D street, twice. About 4 minutes.

AUB Human-Centered Design student project. Anonymous: no name, email, camera or microphone.

What we record, and your code
  • We are testing the directions, not you.
  • We record your answers, the route you walk and where you look in the 3D street (so we can replay it), how long it takes, which buildings you choose, and whether you use a phone or a computer.
  • It's voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page; what was recorded until then is kept unless you ask us to delete it.
  • Your code is . With it we can delete your data later. At the end we explain what we were looking at, and you can delete it yourself.
